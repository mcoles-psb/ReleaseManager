In Supabase, open the project, click Connect and copy the Session pooler connection string, then put the database password in place of [YOUR-PASSWORD]. Special characters in the password (such as @ # / ? %) must be URL-encoded. Saving with a name that already exists replaces that connection. Connections are kept on this computer only, in Config/databaseConnections.json, which is never committed to git. Every check runs read-only.
